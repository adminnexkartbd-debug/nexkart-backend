Penalties & Violations Policy

Overview of enforcement actions, penalties, and platform guidelines

Counterfeit & Fake Products Severe

Listing replica or fake branded products is strictly prohibited. Violators face instant product removal, fine penalty, and immediate seller account termination.

Misleading Product Information Low

Using incorrect specifications, fake images, or misleading titles will result in temporary product delisting until verified details are provided.

Late Order Processing & Cancellation Moderate

Sellers who fail to dispatch orders within the promised timeframe or frequently cancel orders will incur penalty fees per order and reduced search visibility.

Fake Reviews & Rating Manipulation Moderate

Posting fake positive reviews or artificially boosting ratings is banned. Discovered fake reviews will be purged, and store rank will be downgraded.

Off-Platform Transactions & Abuse Severe

Attempting to redirect customers outside NexKart for payments or sending abusive messages will result in an immediate permanent account ban.

Buyer Fraud & Fake Claims Severe

Buyers making fraudulent refund requests or returning damaged/swapped items will be blacklisted and face permanent account suspension.
