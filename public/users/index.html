1. Introduction

Welcome to NexKartBD! Protecting the privacy and security of your personal data is our primary commitment. Whether you use our platform as a Customer or register as a Seller / Vendor, this Privacy Policy outlines how your information is collected, processed, stored, and protected. By using our services, you agree to the terms mentioned in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to operate smoothly for both buyers and sellers on our platform:

A. From Customers:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, mobile number, shipping & billing address.
  • Account Info: Username, encrypted password, wishlist.
  • Payment Data: Order details, chosen payment methods (e.g., bKash, Nagad, Cards). (We do not store card credentials directly).

B. From Sellers / Vendors:

  • Business & Identity Data: Proprietor's name, Shop/Store name, NID or Passport copy, and Trade License (if applicable).
  • Contact Details: Business email address, phone number, shop/warehouse pickup location.
  • Financial Details: Bank account details or Mobile Financial Services (bKash/Nagad) numbers for sales payout.
  • Catalog Data: Product listings, inventory data, and order history uploaded via Seller Panel.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate and functional business needs:

  • To fulfill customer orders, facilitate courier delivery, and issue invoices.
  • To verify and approve authentic Seller Accounts on the platform.
  • To disburse sales earnings (Payouts) to seller bank accounts or mobile wallets accurately.
  • To resolve disputes between customers and sellers regarding orders or products.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraudulent or malicious activities.

4. Data Protection & Security

NexKartBD maintains high-level data protection standards:

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmissions are encrypted using standard SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) technology.
  • Strict Confidentiality: We NEVER sell, rent, or lease customer or seller personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Logistics Sharing: Delivery addresses and contact numbers are strictly shared with authorized logistics/courier partners solely for order fulfillment.

5. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

At NexKartBD, customer satisfaction is our top priority. Our return and refund guidelines include:

  • Return Eligibility: If you receive a damaged, defective, wrong product, or missing item, you can request a return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Item Condition: Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags and invoices included.
  • Refund Process: Once inspected and approved, refunds will be processed to your original payment method (bKash/Nagad/Bank) within 7 to 10 working days.
  • Order Cancellation: Customers can cancel orders free of charge before shipment. Refunds for pre-paid orders will be credited within 5-7 working days.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights (Buyers & Sellers)

Both Customers and Sellers retain complete rights over their account data:

  • Right to Request Deletion: You have the full right to request deletion of your Customer or Seller account at any time.
  • How to Delete: You can send an account deletion request through profile settings or directly email admin.nexkartbd@gmail.com.
  • Data Removal: Upon verification and approval, your profile info, saved addresses, and active records will be permanently removed from our active servers.
  • Seller Clearance Requirement: For Seller Accounts, deletion requires that there are no pending customer orders, unresolved disputes, or unsettled financial payouts.
  • Legal Retention: Transaction records and invoices will be stored for a legally required period to comply with national auditing and tax regulations.
